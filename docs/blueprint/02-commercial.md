# Sections 14–15 — Commercial

## 14. Quotation

Document: **WAREHOUSING / STORAGE QUOTATION**

Fields: quotation number, date, valid until, customer, warehouse, services,
rates, taxes, payment terms, special conditions, notes.

Status: Draft → Sent → Accepted / Rejected / Expired.

After acceptance provide **Create Agreement**, which pre-fills agreement
information.

## 15. Agreement

Document: **WAREHOUSING / STORAGE SERVICE AGREEMENT**

Use a configurable template. Wizard steps:

1. Parties
2. Warehouse
3. Services
4. Goods
5. Commercial terms
6. Rates
7. Payment
8. Liability / insurance
9. Term
10. Termination
11. Signatories

The generated document must automatically use: company master, customer master,
warehouse master, accepted quotation, rate card.

Legal wording must remain editable/configurable and should be treated as a
template requiring appropriate legal review.
