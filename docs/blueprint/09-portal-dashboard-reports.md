# Sections 53–55 — Customer Portal, Dashboard, Reports

## 53. Customer Portal

Customer login must show only their own data.

**Dashboard:** current stock, inward, outward, pending releases, pending POD,
outstanding, recent documents.

**Documents:** GRN, Warehouse Receipt, Dispatch, Gate Pass, POD, Invoice, Stock
Statement, Account Statement. Customers can download documents.

## 54. Dashboard

Keep it professional and useful.

**Cards:** Today's Inward, Today's Outward, Current Stock, Pending GRNs, Pending
POD, Outstanding, Pending Approvals.

**Sections:** Inward vs Outward, Recent Transactions, Pending Approvals, Ageing
Alerts, Payment Alerts.

Do not overload the dashboard with unnecessary charts.

## 55. Reports

**Operations:** Daily Inward, Daily Outward, Gate Entry, GRN, Dispatch.

**Stock:** Current Stock, Customer Stock, Stock Ledger, Stock Ageing, Stock
Movement, Location Stock, Stock Verification, Stock Adjustment.

**Billing:** Storage Charges, Handling Charges, Invoice Register, Outstanding,
Collection, Customer Statement.

**Documents:** Document Register, Pending POD, Pending Approvals, Agreement
Expiry.

**Export:** PDF; Excel/CSV where appropriate.
