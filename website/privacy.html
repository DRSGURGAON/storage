Privacy Policy

StorageBill Pro (Android, package com.drs.godownbook) · Last updated 14 September 2026

This policy explains what StorageBill Pro collects, why, where it is kept, who else can see it, and how to get it deleted. It covers the Android app and this website. Plain words are used on purpose; if anything here is unclear, write to us and we will explain it.

Who we are

StorageBill Pro is published by {{COMPANY_NAME}}, {{COMPANY_ADDRESS}}. For anything in this policy, we are the data controller and you can reach us at the address in Contact.

The short version

  • We ask for your mobile number so you can sign in. That is the only personal detail we require.
  • Your business records live on your phone. The app works fully offline.
  • A backup copy is kept in the cloud so you do not lose your work when you change or reset your phone.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and its cloud copy yourself, from inside the app or by writing to us.

Who processes your data for us

We do not run our own servers. The app uses Google's Firebase platform, which processes data on our behalf:

  • Firebase Authentication verifies your mobile number by SMS and holds your sign-in account.
  • Cloud Firestore holds your backup, your subscription record, your KYC submission and signing links.
  • Google Play services confirms the app on your phone is the genuine app before an SMS code is sent.

Google's own handling of this data is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security page and the Google Privacy Policy. The app also reads our public release page on GitHub to see whether a newer version exists; that request carries no information about you beyond what any web request carries.

Who can see your data

  • You. Your records are readable only by the account that created them. Our security rules refuse one account any access to another's data.
  • Our staff. A small number of administrators can see company profiles, subscription records and KYC submissions in order to activate subscriptions and answer support requests. Every such action is written to an audit log that records who did what and when.
  • Anyone you send a signing link to. A signing link shows that one document's details to whoever opens it. Treat it like a WhatsApp message: send it only to the customer it concerns.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it for advertising. We disclose data only where the law requires it of us.

Payments

Subscriptions are paid by UPI. The app shows a QR code or UPI address and you pay from your own UPI app. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank password. We record only that a payment was claimed against your account, and the reference you give us.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete the record, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud backup: for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account deletes it.
  • Signing links: they expire on their own, and you can cancel one at any time.
  • Subscription and audit records: kept after an account is deleted, because we need them for our own accounting and tax obligations. They stop being reachable by any sign-in once the account is gone.

Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account yourself: open the app, go to Settings, then Delete Account. This removes your sign-in account and your cloud backup.

Records saved on your own phone are deliberately left alone, because they may be bills and receipts you are legally required to keep. To remove those too, uninstall the app or clear its data from your phone's settings.

If you cannot open the app, you can ask us to delete the account instead. The full method, and what is and is not deleted, is on the account deletion page.

Your rights

You may ask us what we hold about you, ask for a correction, ask for deletion, or object to a particular use. Write to the address in Contact from the mobile number on the account, and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer you may complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Security

Data travels to and from our cloud over an encrypted connection. Server-side rules, not just the app, decide who may read or write each record, and they are tested automatically before every release. Administrator access is granted by a signed credential held outside the app, so nobody can grant it to themselves from a phone. No system is perfect; if you believe an account has been misused, tell us at once.

Children

StorageBill Pro is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, write to us and we will remove the account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will change the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, tell you inside the app. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
