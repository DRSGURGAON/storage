Built for how a godown actually runs

Not a general-purpose accounting app with a storage module bolted on. Every screen is one of the jobs a godown owner does in a day.

Goods in, goods out

Record what arrived, whose it is, how many boxes and where it is kept. Release part of it or all of it, and the record updates itself.

Rent that bills itself

Rent runs from the day goods arrive to the day they leave. Bills stop on the release date, and a part release only charges for what is still in storage.

Payments that add up

Take money against a bill, oldest first. A payment larger than the bill becomes an advance instead of a mistake, and the customer statement always agrees with the dashboard.

Works without network

Everything lives on the phone, so the app opens and works in a shed with no signal. When the network returns, a backup goes to the cloud on its own.

Hindi and English on paper

Documents print with a proper Devanagari font, so a customer name or a goods description in Hindi appears exactly as it was typed.

Signed by the customer

Send a link, the customer opens it on their own phone, reads the terms and signs with a finger. The signature prints on the receipt and the agreement.

Your letterhead, every time

Logo, address, GST number, signature and stamp are set once and appear on every document, so nothing looks handmade.

Correct a wrong entry

A receipt, bill or release made by mistake can be edited from its own PDF screen. The number stays the same and everything that depends on it is restated.

Restores on a new phone

Sign in with the same number on a new phone and your company, customers and records come back from the cloud backup.

How it works

Four steps from installing the app to handing over a printed receipt.

  1. Sign in with your number An OTP arrives by SMS. No password to remember, no email needed.
  2. Set up your company once Name, address, GST number, logo and signature. This becomes your letterhead.
  3. Record the goods Customer, items, boxes, rent and the place they are kept. The receipt prints itself.
  4. Bill, collect, release Raise the rent bill, take the payment, release the goods and close the record.

What it costs

The app is free to install and free to try. Every document type can be made a few times at no cost, so you can see your own paper before deciding. After that a subscription unlocks unlimited documents. Prices and payment details are shown inside the app, on the Subscription screen.
