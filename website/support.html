Getting the app

StorageBill Pro runs on Android phones with Android 6.0 or newer. The app is published on Google Play. Until the Play listing is live in your country, you can install the latest build directly:

Download the latest build

If your phone warns about installing from outside the Play Store, allow it for your browser once and the install will continue. Updates arrive inside the app: when a newer build exists, the home screen offers it.

Common questions

The app asks for an OTP but no SMS arrives.

Check that the number you typed is the number in the phone, that it has signal, and that no app is blocking SMS from unknown senders. Wait for the resend timer and tap Resend. If it still does not arrive, write to us with the number and the time you tried.

I changed my phone. Where is my data?

Install the app on the new phone and sign in with the same mobile number. Your company, customers and records restore from the cloud backup on their own. Photographs of goods are kept only on the old phone and do not restore.

Can two people use one account on two phones?

Not at the same time. One account is meant for one phone. Signing in on a second phone restores the backup there, and whichever phone saves last wins. If you need a counter clerk and an owner working together, tell us what you need and we will advise.

I made a receipt with the wrong details.

Open the document, tap the menu on its PDF screen and choose Edit. The number stays the same and anything that depended on it is corrected too. You do not need to delete and start again.

Hindi text is printing as boxes.

It should not. The app bundles a Devanagari font for exactly this. If you see boxes, send us the PDF and tell us which phone you are on.

How do the free documents work?

Each type of document can be created a few times at no cost, so you can print a real receipt and a real bill before paying for anything. The count is shown on the Subscription screen. When a type runs out, only that type is locked.

How do I pay for a subscription?

Open Subscription in the app, scan the UPI QR code shown there and pay from any UPI app. Send us the payment reference and we activate your plan. We never ask for your UPI PIN, card number or bank password. Nobody from our side will ever ask you for an OTP.

How do I close my account?

Settings, then Delete Account. The full explanation of what is removed is on the account deletion page.
