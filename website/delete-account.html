Delete your account and your data

StorageBill Pro (Android, package com.drs.godownbook) · Last updated 14 September 2026

You can delete your StorageBill Pro account at any time, and you do not need our permission or our help to do it. There are two ways: from inside the app, which is immediate, or by writing to us, which is for when you no longer have the app or the phone number.

Method 1 - from inside the app

  1. Open StorageBill Pro and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation and tap Delete Account again.
  5. If you have been signed in for a while, the app asks you to verify your mobile number once more. This is a safety check so that nobody else can delete your account from an unlocked phone.

The deletion happens straight away. There is no waiting period and no approval step.

Method 2 - ask us to delete it

Send an email to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the subject Delete my StorageBill Pro account, and include:

  • the mobile number the account is registered on, with country code;
  • your App ID, such as SW1324, if you know it. It is shown under your company name on the app's home screen.

So that we do not delete the wrong person's records, we will send a confirmation to that same mobile number and wait for your reply before acting. We complete verified requests within 30 days and write back when it is done.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your sign-in account and mobile number Deleted immediately. You can no longer sign in, and the number is free to register again as a new account.
Your company profile in the cloud Deleted immediately.
Your cloud backup: customers, storage records, bills, payments, releases, notices Deleted immediately.
KYC papers you submitted Deleted on request. Tell us in your email if you want these removed as well.
Business records saved on your own phone Left on the phone on purpose. See below.
Your subscription record, and the audit log of administrator actions Kept, because we need them for our own accounting and tax obligations. They no longer carry your sign-in and cannot be reached by any account.
Why your phone's records are not wiped. The receipts, bills and statements on your phone may be documents you are legally required to keep for several years. Deleting your sign-in account must not destroy them for you. When you do want them gone, uninstall the app, or open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then StorageBill Pro, then Storage, and tap Clear data. That erases everything the app holds on that phone and cannot be undone.

Before you delete

  • Deletion cannot be undone. Signing up again with the same number creates a brand new, empty account.
  • An active paid subscription is not refunded by deleting the account. If you want a refund, ask us first.
  • Save any documents you still need. Open each one and share or save the PDF before deleting.
